Privacy & Data Safety

Aroquix is designed with privacy and user respect at its core. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Aroquix mobile game.

1. Offline Gameplay & Local Storage

Your game progress, coin balance, high scores, selected theme, and game settings may be stored locally on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore or similar local storage. Core gameplay and puzzle generation can operate without requiring an account.

2. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

Aroquix may use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements, including rewarded ads and non-intrusive interstitial ads between gameplay sessions. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, advertising information, IP address, and other data as described in Google's applicable privacy documentation and according to your device and consent settings.

Where required by applicable law, consent options may be presented before personalized advertising is used. You may also manage advertising-related preferences through your device settings where available.

3. Data Sharing

Aroquix does not sell your personal information. Information handled by third-party advertising services may be processed or shared by those providers to deliver, measure, secure, and improve advertising services in accordance with their own privacy policies.

4. Internet Access & Permissions

Internet access may be used to load and display ads and related advertising services. Aroquix does not require unnecessary permissions for core gameplay. Permissions requested by the app, if any, are intended only for features supported by the installed version of the app.

5. No In-App Purchases

The current version of Aroquix does not offer in-app purchases. The full core game can be played without buying premium items.

6. Children's Privacy

Aroquix is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us so that we can review and address the matter where applicable.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its features, or applicable requirements change. Any updates will be posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
